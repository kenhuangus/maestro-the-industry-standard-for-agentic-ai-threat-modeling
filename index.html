The Framework

Seven Layers, One Method

MAESTRO expands to Multi-Agent Environment, Security, Threat, Risk, and Outcome.

Definition CSA, 6 February 2025

  • Published by the Cloud Security Alliance on 6 February 2025, for threat modeling multi-agent and agentic AI systems.
  • Seven layers run from the foundation model up to the agent ecosystem — every component of an agent system maps to a layer.
  • It works alongside STRIDE, OWASP classifications and MITRE ATLAS — teams do not discard their existing threat catalogs.
Source: Cloud Security Alliance, MAESTRO framework (6 Feb 2025) — slide 14.

Why Layers Matter

Attacks Cross Layers

A flat checklist misses the chain. MAESTRO's layers exist so a team can follow one attack from entry to outcome.

Chains Follow the propagation

  • The Snyk Labs guide traces one chain end to end: L2 data poisoning → L3 decision-making → L7 unauthorised action.
  • SentinelleIA (mapping July 2025; benchmark series 22 Aug 2026): 9 agents plus a supervisor across all 7 layers — attack success 0.85 direct, 0.00 shielded, in 17 of 20 combinations.
  • AIRQ uses MAESTRO as a coverage validator (section 3.4), mapping 10 attack-surface factors to the layers.

The Method

Six Steps, Start to Report

The CSA method, as the Helmwart wizard implements it — the same six steps, in order, every time.

Method CSA six-step method

  • The six steps: decomposition, per-layer threat identification, cross-layer propagation, risk scoring, mitigation, monitoring.
  • In practice: scope the system, map each component to L1–L7, list threats per layer, then follow one chain across layers.
  • Finish the loop: score likelihood × impact, assign one mitigation per layer, write the threat-modeling report, and re-check after any change.
Sources: Helmwart wizard (CSA six-step method) · Lesson 56 teaches a five-step variant — scope, decompose, enumerate, score, control and re-evaluate · a Udemy case-study course uses four — identify, analyze, prioritize, mitigate, ending in a report — slide 14.

Synthesis

Use It Alongside What You Have

MAESTRO earned the “standard” label by fitting into existing practice, not by replacing it.

Takeaways Three points to keep

  • It complements, not replaces: IriusRisk maps MAESTRO threats to MITRE ATLAS, and the Snyk Labs guide maps the OWASP ASI taxonomy onto the layers.
  • MAESTRO v2 releases 12 January 2027 — two weeks before this keynote at the CSA Virtual Summit on 27 January 2027.
  • Start with one system: map it to the seven layers and follow one cross-layer chain before scaling the method.
One line to remember: “Scope, map to L1–L7, follow one chain across layers, and write the report.”
